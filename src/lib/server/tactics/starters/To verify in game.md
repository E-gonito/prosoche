# To verify in game

These claims about how Ready or Not grades the rules of engagement come
from community talk, not from you. None of them is a scenario until you
have tested it. Once one is confirmed, write it up as an `own-aar` or
`official` scenario and tick it here.

- [ ] Hitting a suspect who already has their hands up with less-lethal again: does that count as unauthorised force?
- [ ] Gas: if a cloud catches a surrendered suspect or a civilian, is that a penalty?
- [ ] A suspect who runs away without ever having fired: does shooting them count as a justified threat, or as unauthorised force?
- [ ] Is a suspect holding a weapon low, but not aiming it, a lethal-force threat before you have shouted a command?
- [ ] Does a suspect who surrenders and then picks a weapon back up reset what force is justified?
- [ ] Does a missed arrest on someone who has surrendered (killed by your AI squad) count against you?
- [ ] Which patch did you check each answer on? Write it next to the line.
