## Situation
You have just used most of a magazine in a fight. You are standing in the open, three metres from a doorway nobody has cleared.

## Options
- A: Reload where you are
- B: Get out of the doorway's line of sight first, then reload
- C: Push through the doorway and reload once you see it is empty
- D: Keep watching the doorway and wait

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> A reload is a few seconds you cannot shoot. Anyone in that doorway sees you the whole time. Out of its line of sight, the worst they can do is come round the corner to you, and you will be loaded when they do.
