## Situation
You are coming up a corridor, keeping to its right wall. It ends in a T junction, with one branch to the left and one to the right. Put the two branches in the order you clear them.

## Map
```tactic-map
{ "v": 1, "grid": [12, 10],
  "walls": [[0,2,12,2],[0,4,5,4],[7,4,12,4],[5,4,5,10],[7,4,7,10]],
  "doors": [],
  "angles": [{"id":"A1","from":[6,4],"dir":"W"},
             {"id":"A2","from":[6,4],"dir":"E"}],
  "entities": [{"type":"player","at":[6.6,8],"state":"right wall"}] }
```

## Answer
A1, A2

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> Along the right wall, you can see past the left-hand corner while you are still well back. So you slice the left branch first from where you are, and cross to the left wall to slice the right branch. Clear the right one first and you have to step out into the junction blind.
