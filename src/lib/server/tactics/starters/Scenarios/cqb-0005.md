## Situation
You have cleared the room ahead and are about to go deeper. Behind you is a side doorway you walked past on the way in. Nobody cleared it.

## Options
- A: Keep going; you walked past it and nothing happened
- B: Clear it, or have it covered, before you go deeper
- C: Go deeper, faster
- D: Turn round and watch it from where you are

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> Walking past a doorway safely tells you nobody was looking out of it then. It does not tell you nobody is in there. Leave it uncleared and anyone who comes out of it is behind you, and you will be facing the other way.
