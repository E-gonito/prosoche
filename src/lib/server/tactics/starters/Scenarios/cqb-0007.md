## Situation
An open door leads into a room you have not cleared. You have cleared as much as you can see from outside.

## Options
- A: Stop in the doorway and scan the room
- B: Go through the doorway without stopping, and clear the corners as you go
- C: Stand just inside the doorway, with your back to the frame
- D: Throw something in and stay outside

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> Every angle in the room can see the doorway, and the frame stops you from moving sideways. Stopping there is the most exposed you can be. Go through it, don't stand in it.
