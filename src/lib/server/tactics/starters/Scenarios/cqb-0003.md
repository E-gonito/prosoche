## Situation
You peeked a long hallway, traded shots and missed. You are back in cover. The enemy saw where you came from and has not moved.

## Options
- A: Peek again from the same spot, straight away
- B: Change your position or your timing, then peek again
- C: Peek again from the same spot, but wider
- D: Peek again from the same spot, crouched

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> They are now aiming exactly where you came out. A, C and D all show up in the spot they are already watching. Any change of position or timing makes them react to you, instead of you walking into their aim.
