## Situation
A corner opens onto a large room. You have heard two players in it, but you don't know where they are standing.

## Options
- A: Swing out wide and see the whole room at once
- B: Slice the corner and take the room a sliver at a time
- C: Hold the corner and wait for them to come to you
- D: Run across the opening to the far side

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> A wide swing shows you to every spot in the room at once, so both of them can shoot while you can only aim at one. Slicing means each sliver you open shows you to as few spots as possible, and you fight them one at a time.
>
> C is not wrong if you have time. It is a different plan, and it is the one to draft as a separate scenario.
