## Situation
You are in the hallway, left of an open door. The room beyond is not cleared. You will slice the pie from outside before you go in. Put the angles in the order they open up to you.

## Map

```tactic-map
  "doors": [{"id":"D1","at":[5,5]}],
  "angles": [{"id":"A1","from":[5,5],"dir":"NE"},
             {"id":"A2","from":[5,5],"dir":"N"},
             {"id":"A3","from":[5,5],"dir":"NW"}],
  "entities": [{"type":"player","at":[2,6.5],"state":"outside"}] }
```

## Answer
A1, A2, A3

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> From the left of the door, your line of sight through it points right, so the far right of the room opens first. Each step to the right swings that line left, across the back wall, until the far left opens last. Taking them in that order means only one new angle can see you at a time.
