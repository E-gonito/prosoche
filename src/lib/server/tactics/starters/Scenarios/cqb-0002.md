## Situation
You are in a room you have cleared. It has two open doorways you have not cleared, one ahead and one to your right. You hear nothing. Where do you stand?

## Options
- A: In the middle of the room, where you can see both doorways
- B: Where only one doorway can see you; hold it, then clear the other
- C: Push the nearer doorway straight away
- D: Stay where you are and wait for a sound

## Answer
B

## Why
> [!suggestion]
> In the middle, both doorways can see you at once, and you can only aim at one. Standing where one doorway's wall blocks the other means anyone coming in meets you one at a time.
